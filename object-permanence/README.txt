OBJECT PERMANENCE  -  how to run it and change it

PREVIEW ON YOUR COMPUTER
  Open Terminal in this folder and run:   python3 serve.py
  Then open http://localhost:8000 and wear headphones.
  (Double-clicking index.html will NOT play sound. The browser blocks it. That is normal.)

PUT IT ON GITHUB
  Copy this whole "object-permanence" folder into your course repo, commit, push.
  Link to it from your index page:  <a href="object-permanence/">Object Permanence</a>
  GitHub Pages serves it at  https://YOURNAME.github.io/REPO/object-permanence/

THE EMPTIED ROOM (Photoshop)
  Save it as  photos/wide_room_empty.jpg  (about 2000 px on the long side, same framing as wide_room.jpg).
  The last wide-room photo uses it automatically. Until then it shows the original.

THE OTHER ENDING
  Add  ?ending=room  to the web address. The room tone comes back over black instead of the street fading out.

TUNE THE LEVELS BY EAR
  Press M on the page. Sliders are in dB on top of your files (0 = exactly as you made them).
  "Jump to" lets you hear any moment. "Copy mix" copies your settings so you can send them to me.

CHANGE THE ORDER, CAPTIONS, OR SOUNDS
  Open index.html in a text editor. The two tables at the top (SOUNDS and STEPS) are the whole piece.

YOUR AUDIO FILES ARE UNCHANGED
  Everything in audio/ is byte-for-byte what you exported from Premiere.
