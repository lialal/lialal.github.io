Project Abstract

Object Permanence is about why we keep what we keep, and what happens to our things once the person they mattered to is gone. It begins in the apartment of a 93 year old, Linda Asher, exploring her relationship to her many objects. Linda died halfway through the project. I examine what, then, to make of her objects? Can a person be revealed through the things they lived with, after they’re gone?

Project Description

Linda raised her kids in this apartment, hosted her sister's wedding here, and died here, in the same place her husband did. Because rent control meant she was never at risk of being priced out, she got to do something most New Yorkers can't: stay. She saved, built, repaired, arranged, imagined, and collected. Everything she owned had a winding story, and entering her space felt like entering a world she had dreamed up.

Rent control isn't the subject of this project so much as the condition that made it possible. Not worrying about the next lease or the next rent check is what lets one person turn an apartment into a self. This project looks at what that produces, alongside what happens when you leave.

Bio
